Quality approach

Make quality part of the work—not a final checkpoint.

Reliable annotation begins with a shared definition of correctness. Review, feedback, and issue resolution should be designed into the workflow from the start.

A practical framework

Clear criteria. Visible review. Better learning loops.

Quality controls should be proportionate to task risk and complexity. Before work begins, teams agree on what is measured, how edge cases are handled, and when a result is accepted or escalated.

Illustrative principle

Quality should be observable, explainable, and repeatable.

This is a proposed operating framework, not a claim of certified processes or measured company performance. Controls are tailored to the engagement and confirmed during project setup.

Review lifecycle

Build a feedback loop into every batch.

The exact checks depend on the task, the source data, and the customer's acceptance requirements.

01 / PREPARE

Define what good looks like

Create guidelines, positive and negative examples, exclusion rules, and a route for ambiguous cases.

02 / CALIBRATE

Align on interpretation

Use a sample batch to surface disagreements, refine instructions, and confirm review criteria.

03 / REVIEW

Check outputs against criteria

Apply the agreed reviewer coverage, validation rules, and escalation path for exceptions.

04 / IMPROVE

Turn issues into learning

Record recurring defects, clarify guidance, and apply approved changes consistently.
