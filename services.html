From brief to delivery

Start small. Learn early. Scale with clarity.

A structured delivery path reduces ambiguity before it becomes rework. Project plans should reflect dataset complexity, acceptance criteria, and the level of review the task requires.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the task

Align on goals, data types, edge cases, formats, and success criteria.

02 / DEFINE

Prepare guidelines

Document taxonomy, examples, exceptions, and decision rules.

03 / PILOT

Validate a sample

Review interpretation and refine the workflow before full execution.

04 / DELIVER

Review and hand over

Check outputs against the agreed criteria and document delivery details.
