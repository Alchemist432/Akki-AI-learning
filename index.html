How we work

Clarity first.
Quality at every step.

Good annotation isn't just labeling at speed. It's a repeatable operation with shared definitions, visible review criteria, and a clear path for resolving edge cases.

01 / ALIGN

Understand the data and goal

Agree on use cases, label taxonomy, edge cases, acceptance criteria, and delivery format.

02 / PILOT

Validate the guidelines

Run a representative sample, review ambiguous cases, and refine instructions before scaling.

03 / EXECUTE

Annotate with review loops

Use clear task ownership, reviewer checks, escalation paths, and progress visibility.

04 / DELIVER

Check, package, improve

Validate outputs against agreed criteria, share delivery notes, and capture lessons for the next batch.

Quality by design

Trust is built into the workflow—not added at the end.
